You will see the dagesh (dot in the middle) in other letters.

That's just for added emphasis.

It only changes the sound of the letters in this chart.

The Final Forms are used when that letter falls at the end of a word.

Final Form letters are also called "sofit" letters.

Such as "mem sofit", "nun sofit"...
